About me

A little more about who I am, not just what I do.

"I'm still an undergraduate, but I show up ready, and I'm open to starting immediately."

I'm Olawumi Owoeye. Content strategist, social media manager, and a law student who never quite fit into one lane.

I make things: reels that stop the scroll, carousels people actually screenshot, captions that sound like a real person wrote them, because one did. I currently handle the Instagram page for the African Writers Conference, an event powered by Writers Space Africa. I also design most of my own graphics in Canva, from promo posts to brand materials, and I edit my videos myself in CapCut.

Alongside the content, I'm studying law. I don't lead with it, but it shapes how I work. I research properly before I write, I don't miss details, and I take deadlines seriously. What genuinely excites me is the space where law, business, creativity and AI meet, and I'm the kind of person who will happily fall down a rabbit hole trying to understand how something works.

I'm curious, detail-oriented and easy to work with, especially with people who have strong ideas and are willing to challenge mine.
